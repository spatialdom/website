| Route | First thought | First action |
| --- | --- | --- |

The pages are driven by `src/data/lguProductPages.json` and share `src/pages/LguProductPage.tsx`. The build writes route-specific HTML, metadata, and sitemap entries for all seven routes. Product cards and related Insights guide visitors into these pages.

SPARTA is described as tax-mapping modernization that may include scanning, data population, reconciliation, parcel mapping, field validation, and maintenance design. The pages do not claim RPVARA compliance. The customer-discovery email asks Assessor's Offices about their current process.

RBIM Cloud is a managed deployment and support offering for the RBIM digital system. The system was developed through a CPD initiative with GIZ assistance; Spatialdom's stated role is cloud deployment, hosting, systems administration, maintenance, and implementation support. Keep that attribution visible on every RBIM page. Use CPD's RBIM guidance when explaining the household-data workflow and privacy responsibilities. Do not add customer names or public pricing without verified approval.
