## Selection principles

The scheduler should not simply draft topics in numeric order.

Prefer a varied batch, while keeping land as the center of gravity. Consider:
- reader/search demand
- freshness and regulatory relevance
- useful connections to Parcel Plotter, SPARTA, RBIM Cloud, PTR, or Spatialdom tools
- variety of audience and article length
- gaps in the currently published library
- recent reader/product questions

During the initial phase, rewrite INSIGHT-001 through INSIGHT-009 before drafting the future seed topics.
